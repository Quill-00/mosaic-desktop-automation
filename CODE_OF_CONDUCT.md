# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Mosaic community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respectful feedback, accepting responsibility, focusing on what is best for the community, and welcoming different viewpoints. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or attention, trolling, insulting comments, threats, publishing private information, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participation for behavior they consider inappropriate. Reports should use the repository's private reporting channel. Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and address incidents fairly.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents the project in public spaces.
